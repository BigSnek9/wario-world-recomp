**Wario World Recomp v1.0**, the first release. Wario World (GameCube, 2003) runs as a native Windows program: the game's own code was translated to C and compiled for the PC, on a GameCube-compatible runtime based on Dolphin. It has been played from start to finish. Everything in this project was made by Claude, Anthropic's AI.

**New**
- One download, no installs: unzip, double-click `WarioWorldRecomp.exe` and pick your own disc. It checks the disc, copies the game files next to the exe and starts in seconds.
- In-game menu on Back (controller) or Tab (keyboard). Every option applies straight away. With everything left at the defaults the game looks and plays like the original.
- Smooth frame rate: 60, 120, 180 or 240 FPS, or match your monitor. The extra frames come from the game's own camera and object positions, not from guessing between pictures.
- Widescreen at any aspect ratio (16:9, 16:10, 21:9, 32:9). The view gets wider instead of stretched, the HUD moves to the screen edges, and menus, speech boxes and the pause book keep their shape.
- Resolution up to 8x, or Auto to match your window or monitor (4K and above).
- HDR on Vulkan, with paper white, peak brightness, highlight boost, white level, saturation, vibrance, contrast, gamma and black level, plus menu brightness.
- HD texture packs are loaded in the background without stutter. Drop them into `user\Load\Textures\GWWE01` (the menu opens the folder for you).
- Audio mixer: master, music and sound effects volume, and an option to mute the pause-menu music.
- Controls: Xbox and PlayStation pads and the keyboard work out of the box. The buttons sit where they were on a GameCube pad (A bottom, B left, X right, Y top). You can rebind every button, turn rumble on or off and set its strength.
- Cheats: jump height, walk through walls, super punches, unlock all stages, and mark all collectables as grabbed.
- Performance mode: the graphics run on their own thread, for slower PCs.
- OpenGL or Vulkan, fullscreen (F11 / Alt+Enter), six menu colour styles and an adjustable menu size.
- F12 saves a bug-report snapshot (a savestate and a screenshot) to `data\reports`.
- The game window shows a gold coin icon, made from your own disc on first run.

**Fixes** (found during the playthrough and testing)
- The whole game runs as native code: no game instruction goes through an interpreter or JIT on the tested routes.
- No white border around the window in fullscreen.
- No afterimages or flickering objects at high frame rates, and quitting is clean.
- The pause-menu music follows the Music volume and the mute option, even while the pause book is open.

**To play:** download `WarioWorldRecomp-v1.0.zip`, unzip it anywhere you own (not Program Files), double-click `WarioWorldRecomp.exe` and pick your own Wario World disc image (USA, GWWE01, a plain 1:1 `.iso`). It sets itself up and starts. Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) only. Your saves and settings are in `user\`.
